I'm a systems engineer and open-source author building **reproducible infrastructure with NixOS**, and a Big Data postgraduate at Glasgow Caledonian University.

My work centres on declarative systems: a personal NixOS fleet spanning laptops, a desktop workstation, a headless server, and WSL; published tooling for secret management, Tailscale, and Nix workflows; and self-hosted game servers with full modpack pipelines. I care about infrastructure that is **reproducible, reviewable, and boring to operate** — configured as code, managed from a SQLite content store, and shipped through CI.

I'm currently completing an **MSc in Big Data Technologies**, focused on machine learning, cloud platforms, and applied analytics — while continuing to publish and maintain open-source Nix tooling.
